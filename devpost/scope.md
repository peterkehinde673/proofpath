# ProofPath

ProofPath turns scattered purchase-dispute evidence into a sourced timeline, a view of what's uncertain or missing, and a clear evidence report.

## The Unique Kernel
It connects each part of a chronological story to the evidence that supports it, while making uncertainty and missing proof visible. It organizes and analyzes evidence without deciding who is right or giving legal advice.

## Who It's For
An everyday consumer trying to organize records from an online purchase or service dispute. Instead of piecing together screenshots and receipts by hand, they want a clear account of what happened and what the records do and do not show.

## The Core Loop
The consumer uploads a small set of records—or opens the fictional demo packet—then reviews an AI-generated timeline, evidence status, gaps or inconsistencies, and a human-readable report.

## Inspiration & Identity
ProofPath is built around a simple product principle: evidence should remain traceable as it becomes a story. Its identity is a calm, source-first workspace that makes complicated records easier to follow without pretending that AI can replace human verification.

## Learning Goal
The project uses a plan-first workflow to practice turning a narrow product idea into a testable AI experience. The implementation deliberately favors a small end-to-end system over a larger feature set.

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a short screen demo, the user opens the fictional online-purchase packet—seller conversation, payment confirmation, order receipt, promised delivery date, and follow-up conversation without clear delivery confirmation—and gets an understandable chronological evidence package. The key moment is seeing the timeline connect events to their sources and point out the unconfirmed delivery and what proof would help.

## The POC Boundary
One responsive web interface, one fictional online-purchase dispute scenario, a small evidence set, AI extraction and analysis, a sourced chronological timeline, supported/unsupported/uncertain and missing-evidence indicators, appropriate contradiction detection, and a readable report. The product explains limits and does not act as a lawyer, judge, or arbiter.

## Later
Handling many case types, production-grade evidence management, and capabilities beyond the single purchase-dispute demonstration.

## Explicitly Cut
- Deciding who is right, guilty, or legally entitled to a remedy: outside ProofPath's role and not something the proof of concept can establish.
- A broad multi-purpose case platform: would dilute the single demo and exceed the 2–4 hour target.
