Build With AI · Scope review · Draft

ProofPath makes a messy purchase dispute easier to follow.

A small proof of concept turns a handful of records into a source-linked chronology, calls out uncertainty and evidence gaps, and produces a readable report. It organizes evidence; it does not decide who is right or give legal advice.

One demo, one clear question

A fictional online purchase: the buyer has seller messages, payment confirmation, an order receipt, a promised delivery date, and a follow-up where delivery is not clearly confirmed.

Target: 2–4 hours
The proof

Evidence → Timeline → Missing proof → Report

The user supplies a small packet (or opens the included demo packet). Each extracted event should point back to its source so the user can inspect why it appears in the story.

01 · InputEvidence files

Small demo set: messages, payment, receipt, delivery promise, follow-up.

02 · AnalyzeAI extraction

Find dates, events, people, amounts, claims, and source references.

03 · OrganizeEvidence-linked timeline

Chronological events with a visible link to the record behind each one.

04 · AssessSupported / Uncertain / Missing evidence

Show what records support, what remains unclear, and what could help verify it.

05 · CompareContradictions when detected

Flag a mismatch only when the submitted records actually conflict.

06 · ShareGenerated evidence report

A clear, neutral account that preserves uncertainty and points to sources.

Keep the experiment coherent

Now, later, and cut

Now

One responsive web flow; one fictional purchase packet; a small evidence upload/demo set; extraction and analysis; source-linked timeline; evidence statuses, gaps, appropriate contradictions; readable report.

Later

More dispute types, larger evidence collections, and a broader evidence-management product.

Cut

Legal advice, verdicts, and claims that the system can determine who is right. Those are outside the purpose and proof of this demo.

What is sharp

A memorable through-line

The delivery question makes the demo concrete: the records confirm the purchase and a promise, but do not clearly establish delivery. Source links and visible uncertainty give the timeline a distinct purpose.

What still needs a practical boundary

AI and file handling can expand fast

Real extraction across arbitrary PDFs, screenshots, and pasted text is a lot for 2–4 hours. The spec should constrain the sample packet and upload path, make analysis failures recoverable, and be explicit about what is truly AI-generated versus prepared demo content.

Scope is the approved direction for the proof of concept; detailed screens and behaviors belong in the PRD. Markdown is canonical: scope.md.
